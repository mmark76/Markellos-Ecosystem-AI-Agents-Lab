SIMPLE START GUIDE

Start AI Training Lab

A short guide for your first use on Windows.

Before you begin: You need a Windows computer, an Internet connection for the first setup, and at least 3 GiB of free disk space.

Start in three steps

  1. Download AI-Agents-Lab-Windows.zip from this dashboard. You do not need a GitHub account.
  2. Extract the ZIP into a folder such as Documents.
  3. Double-click Start AI Training Lab.bat. Easy Start checks your computer and asks separately before installing Python, Ollama, or the first local AI model. It starts the Lab automatically when ready.

Ready: Choose your installed model and begin Local Agent Training: Trainer → Student → Evaluator → Improver.

Agent Collaboration is under construction and will be available in a future release. Advanced model training is temporarily locked in this release.
